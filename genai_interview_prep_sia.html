Start here

Tell me about yourself and one GenAI solution you built.

Target 90 seconds to 2 minutes. Cover your background, business problem, architecture, your contribution, evaluation, and why this role interests you.

Suggested structure
  1. Who you are and your relevant experience.
  2. One project and the real user/business problem.
  3. Architecture and why you selected it.
  4. Evaluation results, limitations, and trade-offs.
  5. Why Sia Partners and consulting-style delivery appeal to you.
Be ready for: What did you personally implement? What was tested? What is prototype versus deployed? What would you improve next?
